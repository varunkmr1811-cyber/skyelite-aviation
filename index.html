FAQ

Frequently Asked

How early should I book my flight?
+

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for the best aircraft availability. However, we accommodate last-minute requests whenever possible — our fleet is always on standby.

Do you provide international flights?
+

Yes. SkyElite offers routes across Europe, Asia, America, the Middle East, and beyond. If you can name a destination, we can create the route.

Can I customize onboard services?
+

Absolutely. Custom menus, fine wines, in-flight entertainment, cabin temperature, lighting — every detail is yours to define. Simply tell your concierge.

Are pets allowed onboard?
+

Yes. SkyElite offers pet-friendly private jet experiences for selected routes. Your beloved companion deserves the same luxury treatment.
